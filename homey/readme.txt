AirBee is an ultra-low-power air quality sensor. View CO₂, temperature, humidity and air pressure in Homey, follow changes in Insights and use the measurements in your Flows.

AirBee sends measurements every five minutes using Zigbee or Bluetooth LE, with one wireless mode active at a time. Requires Homey Pro with software 13.4 or newer. Bluetooth requires support for Bluetooth advertisement reception.
